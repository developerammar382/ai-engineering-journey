# Brewly Policies

## Refunds
If your drink is wrong or you don't like it, tell us within 15 minutes and we will make a new one for free. We don't give cash refunds.

## Loyalty card
Every drink gets one stamp. After 9 stamps, your 10th drink is free. Stamps expire after 6 months.

## Large orders
For orders of more than 15 drinks, please call us at least one day before. We give a 10% discount on large orders.

## Studying and working
You are welcome to study or work in the shop. On weekends, please limit your stay to 3 hours when the shop is busy.
