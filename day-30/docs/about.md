# About Brewly

Brewly is a small coffee shop in Lahore, opened in 2021 by two friends, Sara and Bilal.

We are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. On Sundays we open later, from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Our address is 24 Main Boulevard, Gulberg, Lahore. Free parking is available behind the building.

We have free Wi-Fi for customers. Ask the staff for the password, it changes every week.

Pets are welcome on our outdoor terrace, but not inside the shop.
